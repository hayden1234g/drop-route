Settings: live traffic

Without a key, routes use typical speeds (free OSRM + OpenStreetMap). For live traffic, paste a free Mapbox public token (starts with pk.). Create one at mapbox.com → Account → Tokens. Live traffic works for up to 9 stops at a time.

Late drop-offs count 5× against a route: being 1 min late weighs the same as 5 extra minutes of driving. Order data stays on this device (cleared after 14 hours). Addresses go to the map service to be located and routed.
